# TransTeam

TransTeam la workspace chuyen de de dich truyen Trung sang tieng Viet, uu tien nguon tu `69shuba`.

Muc tieu cua team:
- Luu tru thong tin tung bo truyen theo cau truc ro rang.
- Giu glossary, ten rieng va xung ho nhat quan giua cac chuong.
- Tach rieng van ban nguon, ban dich nhap, ban bien tap va ban chot.
- Ho tro quy trinh lam viec thuc te khi nguon web co chong bot hoac can copy thu cong.

## Cau truc

- `Knowledge/` - kien thuc nen cho viec dich, profile nguon, quy uoc ten rieng.
- `Rules/` - tieu chuan dich, xu ly nguon, checklist chat luong.
- `Workflows/` - quy trinh tao bo moi, dich chuong, ra ban cuoi.
- `Projects/` - moi bo truyen la mot project rieng.
- `Output/` - noi tap hop cac ban da san sang doc hoac xuat ban noi bo.

## Nguyen tac van hanh

- Nguon uu tien: `https://www.69shuba.com/`
- Khi web chan bot, su dung mot trong ba cach:
  - User dua link chuong cu the de xu ly thu cong.
  - User paste van ban goc vao file raw text.
  - User luu HTML/trang web roi dua vao `Projects/<TenBo>/01_Source_Raw/`.
- Khong dich thang vao ban cuoi. Luon di qua 4 lop:
  - nguon goc
  - ban tach text
  - ban dich nhap
  - ban bien tap chot

## Cach dung nhanh

1. Tao mot thu muc moi trong `Projects/` theo mau `_Project_Template`.
2. Dien metadata bo truyen, link nguon, ten Han/Viet/Anh neu co.
3. Luu text goc theo tung chuong.
4. Lap glossary nhan vat, canh gioi, dia danh, he thong.
5. Dich chuong vao thu muc draft.
6. Bien tap lai de doc tu nhien, giu logic va nhip van.
7. Dua ban final vao `Final_Translation/` va cap nhat `Progress_Index.md`.

## Ghi chu

Trong moi dot lam viec, neu ban dua:
- link bo truyen
- ten bo truyen
- chuong bat dau / ket thuc

thi co the dung `TransTeam` de di tiep ngay tren dung cau truc nay.
