# Naming and Terminology

## Ten nhan vat

- Uu tien Han Viet neu bo truyen theo phong vi tien hiep, huyan huyan, tu chan.
- Uu tien phien am mem neu bo truyen theo webnovel do thi, hoc duong, hien dai.
- Khong doi style giua chung bo tru khi co ly do rat manh.

## Ten canh gioi

- Viet hoa dong nhat tung tang.
- Tach ro:
  - canh gioi lon
  - tieu canh gioi
  - vien man / hau ky / dinh phong

## Ten he thong

- Neu la ten rieng cua mot UI/ability, viet hoa co chu dich.
- Neu la mo ta chuc nang thong thuong, uu tien dich ro nghia.

## Xung ho

- Tranh dich qua sach vo duyen.
- Can theo:
  - tuoi tac nhan vat
  - vi the
  - tinh cach
  - boi canh co trang / hien dai

## Nhip cau

- Ban dich phai doc duoc nhu mot chuong truyen Viet, khong phai mot ban chep nghia.
- Neu cau goc qua dai, duoc phep tach cau de doc muot hon.
