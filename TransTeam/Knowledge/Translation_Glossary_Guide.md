# Translation Glossary Guide

## Muc tieu

Dam bao ten rieng, canh gioi, cong phap, to chuc va cach xung ho duoc dich nhat quan tren toan bo mot bo truyen.

## Nhom glossary chinh

- Nhan vat

- Ky nang / cong phap
- Bao vat / trang bi
- He thong xung ho

## Nguyen tac uu tien

1. Uu tien tinh nhat quan truoc tinh my mieu.
2. Ten da chot trong `Glossary.md` khong doi tuy tien.
3. Neu mot term chua chac, danh dau `CHO_XAC_NHAN`.
4. Neu term da pho bien trong dong tien hiep/huyen huyen, co the giu cach Viet hoa thong dung.
5. Neu ten can giu am Han Viet de giu chat, ghi ro ly do.
