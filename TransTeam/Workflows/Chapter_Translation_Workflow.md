# Chapter Translation Workflow

## Muc tieu

Bien mot chuong nguon thanh mot ban dich co the doc tot bang tieng Viet.

## Quy trinh

1. Dua raw source vao `01_Source_Raw/`.
2. Clean text va luu vao `02_Source_Clean/`.
3. Doi chieu glossary truoc khi dich.
4. Dich nhap vao `03_Draft_Translation/`.
5. Tu bien tap lai de van phong tu nhien.
6. Chuyen ban dat yeu cau sang `04_Final_Translation/`.
7. Cap nhat `Progress_Index.md`.

## Khi clean text

- Bo header/footer rac
- Bo dong quang cao
- Giu lai tieu de chuong
- Giu thu tu doan
- Danh dau cho nghi ngo thay vi doan bua

## Khi dich

- Uu tien nghia va nhip
- Kiem soat xung ho
- Giu thong tin cap do, ten ky nang, he thong
- Neu mot term chua chot, danh dau va quay lai sau
