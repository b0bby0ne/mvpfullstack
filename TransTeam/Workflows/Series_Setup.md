# Series Setup Workflow

## Muc tieu

Tao project moi cho mot bo truyen truoc khi bat dau dich.

## Cac buoc

1. Tao thu muc moi trong `Projects/` dua tren `_Project_Template`.
2. Doi ten thu muc theo ten bo truyen.
3. Dien `Metadata.md`.
4. Dien `Glossary.md` voi nhung term da xac nhan.
5. Tao dong mo dau trong `Progress_Index.md`.
6. Luu link nguon va text nguon dau vao `01_Source_Raw/`.

## Toi thieu can co

- Ten bo truyen
- Link nguon tong
- Quy uoc dich ten
- Quy uoc xung ho
- Trang thai khoi dong

## Ket qua

Sau buoc nay, bo truyen san sang de vao workflow dich chuong.
