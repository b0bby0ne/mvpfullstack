# Quality Checklist

## Truoc khi chot mot chuong

- Tieu de da dung format chua
- So chuong da khop chua
- Ten nhan vat da dung glossary chua
- Canh gioi / cong phap / dia danh da nhat quan chua
- Thoai da tu nhien chua
- Van phong co bi dich may khong
- Co cau nao bo nghia hoac lap nghia khong
- Co doan nao thieu chu, nhay dong, dinh rac web khong
- Nhip doan co de doc khong
- Da cap nhat `Progress_Index.md` chua

## Dau hieu can sua

- Cau qua sat nghia
- Nhieu "han", "na", "cai nay", "cai kia" vo huong
- Xung ho thay doi lien tuc
- Ten rieng luc Viet luc pinyin
- Giong van bi tho, rat giong OCR hoac machine translation
