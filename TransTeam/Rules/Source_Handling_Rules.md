# Source Handling Rules

## Nguyen tac

- Luon luu ban nguon truoc khi clean.
- Moi chuong nen co thong tin xuat xu ro rang.
- Khong tron raw text va ban dich vao cung mot file.

## Thu tu xu ly

1. Luu link nguon vao metadata.
2. Luu raw HTML hoac raw text.
3. Tach text sach.
4. Dich nhap.
5. Bien tap.
6. Chot final.

## Khi nguon web chan bot

- Cho phep workflow thu cong.
- Ghi ro:
  - ngay lay du lieu
  - cach lay du lieu
  - chuong nao da clean

- `Chapter_0001_Raw.md`

- Moi chuong phai co trang thai:
  - `raw`

  - `drafted`
  - `reviewed`
  - `final`
