# Knowledge: Bob Volman Canon

## Muc tieu
Nap bo tri thuc cot loi cua phuong phap Bob Volman vao `SwingAgent` theo dang knowledge base co cau truc.

## Luu y pham vi
- File nay la ban tong hop van hanh, khong chep nguyen van noi dung sach.
- Noi dung duoc to chuc lai de phuc vu doc chart, chon setup va loc swing.
- Muc tieu la "usable knowledge", khong phai ban sao noi dung co ban quyen.

## Canon sach tieng Anh
1. `Forex Price Action Scalping` (2011)
   - trong tam: scalping, tick chart, setup entries, tipping point, trade selection, account management
2. `Understanding Price Action` (2014)
   - trong tam: 5-minute price action, double pressure, practical analysis, manual exits, study process

## Truc kien thuc xuyen suot
- Price truoc, indicator sau.
- Context truoc, pattern sau.
- Khong co double pressure thi khong co trade.
- Break chi duoc giao dich khi chat luong break dat yeu cau.
- Pullback chi duoc giao dich khi cau truc truoc do con hop le.
- Quan ly lenh phai dua tren cau truc gia, khong dua tren hy vong.
- Khong giao dich chi vi "co setup"; phai co boi canh, duong chay va vo hieu ro.
- Hoc bang screen time va chart review, khong hoc bang mot lan doc.

## Khac biet chinh giua 2 cuon

### Forex Price Action Scalping
- nghieng ve execution va setup taxonomy
- dung he tick chart de timing rat sat
- nhan manh 7 setup cot loi
- co tipping point technique va bo loc unfavorable conditions

### Understanding Price Action
- nghieng ve nen tang ly thuyet va chart reading
- dung 5-minute chart de xay dung kha nang doc cau truc
- nhan manh 7 nguyen ly price action
- mo rong sang manual exits, skipping trades, study process, low volatility adaptation

## Cach SwingAgent su dung canon nay
1. Dung `Bob_Volman_Principles.md` de doc context.
2. Dung `Bob_Volman_Setup_Catalog.md` de map pattern sang ten setup.
3. Dung `Bob_Volman_Execution_Management.md` de quyet dinh entry, stop, exit va skip.
4. Dung `Bob_Volman_Training_Risk.md` de danh gia muc tin cay, quy mo va ky luat.

## Dinh huong cho repo nay
- `PriceAgent` la lop thu thap va chuan hoa du lieu.
- `SwingAgent` la lop doc cau truc theo Bob Volman.
- Khi chua co intraday bar dung chuan, `SwingAgent` chi duoc dung knowledge nay o muc `context_only`.
