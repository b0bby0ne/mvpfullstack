# Knowledge: Bob Volman Principles

## 1. Tu duy goc
- Thi truong duoc doc qua hanh vi gia, khong qua indicator chong cheo.
- Moi bar chi co y nghia khi dat vao boi canh truoc no.
- Muc tieu khong phai du doan tuong lai, ma la tham gia khi xac suat nghieng ro ve mot phia.
- Gia di chuyen vi can bang mua ban thay doi; trader phai doc dau vet cua su thay doi do.

## 2. Chart va boi canh

### Trong `Forex Price Action Scalping`
- trong tam la tick chart
- public summaries va reviews gan voi chart `70-tick`
- EMA ngan duoc dung nhu tham chieu boi canh, khong phai trigger tu dong

### Trong `Understanding Price Action`
- trong tam la `5-minute` chart
- chart sach, uu tien naked chart
- EMA ngan (25-EMA) la duong tham chieu cau truc, khong phai indicator phat lenh
- Su tuong tac giua gia va EMA (Magnet effect, The Squeeze) la chia khoi dong cua Breakout.

## 3. Double pressure
- Day la nguyen ly quan trong nhat.
- Mot trade chi hop le khi co it nhat hai luc ky thuat cung ung ho cung mot huong.
- Vi du cac luc co the hoi tu:
  - trend direction
  - support hoac resistance co y nghia
  - buildup sat barrier
  - false break vua xay ra
  - higher low hoac lower high
  - retest cua barrier vua bi break
- Luat van hanh: neu khong co hoi tu, khong vao lenh.

## 4. Support va resistance
- Khong phai mot gia tri chinh xac tuyet doi; day la zone.
- Muc y nghia tang len khi level duoc test nhieu lan.
- Swing high, swing low, bien range va muc tron la cac level uu tien.
- Barrier co the la ngang, co the la mot cuc tri gan, co the la cum gia tao "tran" hoac "san" tam thoi.

## 5. False break, tease break, proper break

### False break
- Gia vuot level nhung khong follow-through.
- Thuong tao trap cho phe vao breakout qua som.
- Rat gia tri khi nam dung vi tri, vi no tao luc o huong nguoc lai. Khi ket hop voi EMA, no dung de danh nguoc ve phia EMA dang chi.

### Tease break
- Break co ve hua hen nhung build-up chua du chuan, thuong chua bi nen chat giua barrier va EMA.
- Thuong la break som, de bi hut nguoc vao level nhay cam hoac bi Magnet effect keo ve EMA.
- Nhiem vu cua trader la phan biet no voi proper break.

### Proper break
- Break xuat hien sau buildup hop ly (hoan hao nhat la Squeeze giua Barrier va 25-EMA).
- Co boi canh ung ho va con duong chay ro.
- Co kha nang tiep dien tot hon vi da co su nen ap luc truoc khi pha (giong hieu ung Pac-Man can dan rao can).

## 6. False highs va false lows
- Mot break cua mot bar hoac mot cuc tri nho co the that bai ngay lap tuc.
- Khi break nho that bai o diem nhay cam, no tro thanh thong tin cuc ky gia tri.
- False high o khang cu va false low o ho tro thuong bao hieu phan ung nguoc huong.
- Trong buildup, false high/low co the la dau hieu dong thuan dang doi phia.

## 7. Pullback reversal
- Pullback khong tu dong la co hoi.
- Pullback chi hop le khi:
  - trend truoc do ro
  - pullback khong pha vo cau truc trend
  - tai diem pullback co level ky thuat ho tro
  - bar phan ung cho thay phe doi huong dang yeu di
- Pullback reversal la cau noi giua continuation va entry gia tot.

## 8. Ceiling test
- Sau mot break som hoac mot arch nho, thi truong thuong quay lai test "tran" hoac "san" trung gian moi hinh thanh.
- Day la mot dang magnet effect.
- Ceiling test giup giai thich vi sao nhieu trader vao break qua som hoac vao retest qua so som bi rung ra.
- Su co mat hoac vang mat cua ceiling test giup danh gia chat luong tiep dien.

## 9. Round number effect
- Muc tron co suc hut tam ly va thuong lam thay doi toc do di chuyen cua gia.
- Khong duoc giao dich chi vi round number.
- Round number la mot thanh phan context, dung de:
  - du doan obstruction
  - du doan phan ung
  - danh gia target co con duong chay hay khong

## 10. Trend, range, transition
- Trend: higher highs/higher lows hoac lower highs/lower lows, cac break duoc xac nhan.
- Range: overlap nhieu, break khong follow-through, bien tren duoi ro.
- Transition: luc trend yeu di nhung range chua xac lap on dinh.
- Bob Volman uu tien giao dich o diem chuyen tu can bang sang mat can bang, khong uu tien giao dich o giua vung chop.

## 11. Buildup
- Buildup la su nen gia sat mot level quan trong.
- Cang chat, cang sat barrier, cang ro y nghia.
- Buildup hop le lam tang xac suat cho proper break.
- Buildup xau thuong qua rong, qua lon xon, hoac nam sai ben canh cua level.

## 12. Tinh chon loc
- Khong phai moi pattern deu duoc trade.
- Mot pattern dep trong boi canh xau van la trade kem.
- Volman uu tien:
  - boi canh ro
  - trap ro
  - duong chay ro
  - vo hieu ro
  - momentum khong bi can ngay phia truoc

## 13. Ky luat voi Chop / Whipsawing
- Chop (Whipsawing) la luc EMA dang nam ngang hoan toan va gia di xuyen len/xuong lien tuc qua EMA ma khong co cau truc ro rang.
- Day la vi tri dot tien nhieu nhat. 
- Nguyen tac: Neu bieu do mang tinh random (overlap the tich lon, thieu double pressure), trade tot nhat la khong vao lenh (Skipping Trades).
