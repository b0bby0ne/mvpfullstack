# Knowledge: Bob Volman Training and Risk

## 1. Time to study, time to trade
- Mot thong diep xuyen suot la phai tach rieng viec hoc va viec danh that.
- Trader moi khong nen vua hoc pattern vua nang tay.
- Chart review, replay va note la mot phan cua he thong, khong phai viec phu.

## 2. Deliberate practice
- Xem lai session lien tiep, khong chi xem nhung chart "dep".
- Danh dau:
  - setup hop le
  - setup suyt hop le
  - setup bi skip
  - setup thua do context hay do execution
- Muc tieu la tao pattern memory.

## 3. Consecutive chart study
- Cuon `Understanding Price Action` nhan manh viec xem chuoi phien lien tiep.
- Day la cach de hieu:
  - khi nao market de trade
  - khi nao market dang khong xung dang de trade
  - setup xuat hien trong dong cau truc lon hon nhu the nao

## 4. Trade size
- Quy mo lenh la mot quyet dinh chien luoc.
- Chi tang quy mo khi:
  - execution on dinh
  - journal cho thay edge du lap lai
  - trader khong con phan ung cam xuc qua muc voi lo/loi
- Khong dung size lon de sua mot he thong chua co edge.

## 5. Compounding
- Tu duy compounding chi hop le khi process on dinh.
- Neu edge chua on dinh, compounding chi lam drawdown lon hon.

## 6. Low volatility adaptation
- Khong ep market it bien dong phai cho setup nhu market binh thuong.
- Khi volatility thap:
  - giam ky vong
  - giam tan suat
  - tang do kho tinh chon loc
  - san sang khong trade

## 7. Probability principle
- Mot trade tot van co the lo.
- Edge duoc danh gia tren tap trade, khong tren mot lenh.
- Ky luat khong nam o viec doan dung, ma o viec lap lai dung quy trinh.

## 8. Words of caution
- Overtrading pha huy edge nhanh hon setup xau.
- Discretion khong co nghia la tuy hung.
- Volman la phuong phap demanding:
  - can tap trung cao
  - can tu choi trade xau
  - can chap nhan lo nho lap di lap lai
- Neu du lieu nguon khong dung granularity, khong duoc vo ep day thanh Bob Volman intraday.

## 9. Rule cho SwingAgent
- Khong duoc xuat setup chi dua tren ten pattern.
- Khong duoc xuat setup neu khong tim thay double pressure.
- Khong duoc xuat setup neu market dang o low-volatility chop ma chua co break chat.
- Khong duoc nang muc tin cay neu du lieu moi o `context_only`.
